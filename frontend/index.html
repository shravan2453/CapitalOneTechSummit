How we optimize your loan plan
Our Strategy: Federal First
  1. Direct Subsidized — Best option! Government pays interest while in school.
  2. Direct Unsubsidized — Same low federal rate, but interest accrues.
  3. State Loans — NC Assist, FELS, etc. Often competitive rates with forgiveness options.
  4. Private Loans — Only to fill remaining gap. Higher rates, fewer protections.

This hierarchy minimizes your long-term cost because federal loans have:

  • Lower, fixed interest rates (currently 6.53% for undergrads)
  • Income-driven repayment options (SAVE plan = 5% of discretionary income)
  • Loan forgiveness after 20-25 years on IDR plans
  • Deferment and forbearance protections
